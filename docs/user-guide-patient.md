# MuVi Rehab: guide for patients

This guide shows you how to do a practice session at home. A family member or carer can read it with you.

MuVi Rehab uses your webcam to follow your arm and hand. **Your video stays on your computer.** Only numbers about your movement are saved, never pictures.

> **Screenshots:** each `[Screenshot: …]` below marks where a picture of that screen will go.

## What you need

- A computer with a webcam, and Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge.
- A chair without arms, about 1 to 1.5 metres from the screen.
- Your login (email and password) from your therapist.
- About 30 minutes.

## 1. Set up your space

- **Sit 1 to 1.5 metres from the screen.**
- **Face a light,** such as a window or a lamp. Do not sit with a bright window behind you.
- **Keep your shoulders and your arm in view.**
- **Use a plain background** if you can.
- Wear short or close-fitting sleeves.

[Screenshot: a person sitting in the right position in front of the computer]

## 2. Log in

1. Open MuVi Rehab in Chrome or Edge.
2. Click **Log in**, type your email and password, and click **Log in** again.

You now see your **home page**: today's plan, your streak, your weekly goal, the last four weeks and your progress.

[Screenshot: the patient home page]

## 3. Start a session

Click **Start session**.

At the top of the screen you can always see where you are: **Setup • Calibrate • Warm-up • Games • Cool-down**. The step you are on is orange.

If you stopped a session earlier today, you will see **Continue your session?** Choose:

- **Continue your session** to carry on where you stopped, or
- **Start over** to begin a new session.

[Screenshot: the "Continue your session?" choice]

## 4. Camera check (Setup)

1. Click **Allow camera**. If the browser asks, click **Allow**.
2. Sit back until the checklist is all green: **Camera on**, **Enough light**, **Shoulders in view** and your arm in view.
3. Click **Continue to calibration**.

**Camera tips**

- **The checklist will not turn green?** Move a little further back and turn on another light.
- **"We could not find a camera"?** Check the camera is plugged in, close other programs that use it (such as video calls), and click **Try again**.
- **The camera was blocked?** Click the camera icon in the address bar, choose **Allow**, and reload the page.

[Screenshot: the camera check with the green checklist]

## 5. Calibration

MuVi Rehab measures how far you can comfortably reach, so the games fit you.

1. Read the instructions and click **Start**.
2. Stars appear around you on the screen. Reach towards each star with your affected arm and hold until it lights up.
3. Reach only as far as is comfortable. **Keep your back against the chair.**
4. At the end you see your **reach map**. A dashed line shows your reach from last time.

[Screenshot: the calibration stars]
[Screenshot: the reach map after calibration]

## 6. Warm-up

There are six short exercises. Before each one you can read the steps or click **Show me** to watch a demonstration.

1. Click **Got it**, and wait for the countdown.
2. Do the movement slowly. The ring counts your repetitions and the gauge shows how far you moved.
3. If you see **Check your posture**, sit up straight and move only your arm.
4. Need a break? Click **Pause**. Too hard? Click **Make it easier**. You can also click **Skip this exercise**.
5. Rest for 20 seconds between exercises.

Your goals change a little each time, based on how your last warm-up went.

If you stopped partway through, the warm-up carries on from the next unfinished exercise.

[Screenshot: a warm-up exercise with the rep counter and gauge]

## 7. Games

1. On **Today's games**, you can untick a game or change the order. Click **Start**.
2. Each game counts down from 3, then begins.
3. Move your hand to play. To "squeeze", close your hand.
4. If your hand leaves the camera view, the game pauses and carries on by itself.
5. After each game you see your score. Click **Next game**, or **Continue** after the last one.

| Game | What you do |
| --- | --- |
| **Reach and squeeze** | Reach to the target, hold, then squeeze. |
| **Balloon pop** | Touch the balloons when they stop rising. |
| **Fruit catch** | Move the basket left and right to catch the falling fruit. |

[Screenshot: a game in progress]

## 8. Cool-down

1. Rest your arm in your lap and breathe slowly with the circle for one minute. You can click **Skip to the questions**.
2. Answer the questions by clicking the big buttons:
   - How much pain do you feel now? (0 to 10)
   - How tired do you feel now? (0 to 10)
   - How much did you enjoy today's session? (1 to 5 stars)
   - How hard was today's session?
   - Did you notice something new or unusual in your body?
3. You can add a comment, then click **Send answers and finish**.

Please answer honestly. Your therapist uses your answers to plan your practice.

[Screenshot: the cool-down questions]

## 9. Your summary

The summary shows what you did today:

- **Time spent moving:** the time you spent on exercises and games.
- **Warm-up goals reached:** for example "5 of 6".
- **Warm-up repetitions:** how many full movements you made.
- **A card for each game:** your score, how many targets you reached, and the game's main measure.
- **Your reach today:** your reach map, with last time's map dashed.

Under many figures you see a short line:

- **Better than last time**
- **About the same as last time**
- **A little lower than last time.** This is normal. Every day is different.

The **Your feedback** box will show personal feedback in a later version.

Click **Back to home** when you are done.

[Screenshot: the session summary]

## Safety

> **Stop if anything hurts.** You can click **Exit** at the top of the screen at any time. In a game, click **Pause** first, then **Exit**.

If you report pain or something new or unusual, the summary shows this message:

> **Please stop practising for today and tell your therapist how you feel. If you notice sudden weakness, numbness, face drooping or difficulty speaking, call emergency services immediately.**

Your therapist also sees your answers.

MuVi Rehab is a practice tool. It does not diagnose anything, and it does not replace your therapist.

## Your progress

- **Streak:** how many days in a row you have finished a session.
- **This week:** the ring fills as you reach the weekly goal your therapist set.
- **Last 4 weeks:** a tick on each day you practised.
- **Progress** (in the menu): larger charts of your reach, warm-up goals and game targets, and your first and latest reach maps.
- **History:** every session, with its details.

## Problems?

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| A message "Something went wrong — your progress is saved" | Click **Back to home**, then **Continue your session**. |
| A yellow bar "You are offline" | Check your internet. Things you do now may not be saved until it is back. |
| The page reloaded | It opens at the step you were on. |
| You forgot your password | Ask your therapist to set a new one. |
